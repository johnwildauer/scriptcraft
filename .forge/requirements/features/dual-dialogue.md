# Requirements Area: Dual Dialogue

> This document specifies desired user-facing behavior for a cohesive area. Describe requirements in established-state voice, even when implementation is future work. Group related capabilities into sections and keep stable IDs so each requirement and acceptance criterion remains independently traceable. Do not add lifecycle status, implementation evidence, progress, or a backlog here.

## Area purpose

Writers can edit a supported two-sided dialogue passage as a bounded group while retaining ordinary screenplay paragraphs, safe editing, predictable pagination, and correct print/export output.

## Capability groups

### Group editing and source preservation

- **REQ-WRITING-LOOP-013: Bounded dual-dialogue editing and Print layout.** The system shall represent supported two-sided dialogue as a grouping over ordered screenplay elements, preserve its supported FDX source, and keep transient editor presentation separate from print output and document state.
  - **AC-WRITING-013.1 — Group shape and toggle eligibility:** A group contains exactly two elements with the exact Character type capability in screenplay order, each followed by zero or more elements whose exact types have the Dialogue or Parenthetical capability. Either side may be empty, which is distinct from a missing paragraph. A third Character-capability element or any other type ends the candidate, and adjacent groups remain distinct. No visible placeholder is added. Toggle enables only a complete, exact two-cue pattern and disables the group containing a collapsed caret. Noncollapsed selections, title-page positions, incomplete/malformed patterns, and ambiguous targets do nothing and do not change document/history state.
  - **AC-WRITING-013.2 — Transaction and structural editing:** Enable/disable preserves member order, identities, canonical text, runs, and direct formatting. Structure-preserving edits retain the group. A structure-breaking edit removes any affected grouping and performs the ordinary edit as one reversible operation; a Type-only change that makes a group's member pattern invalid instead retains its source container and merely deactivates its two-column projection. Failed edits do not partially change content, selection, history, or save state. Text, paste, run-style, and alignment edits preserve the group. Removing the last Dialogue/Parenthetical on a side leaves that side empty and grouped; removing the last right-side Dialogue from a two-cue group retains both Character cues. Enter at the end of the final right Dialogue resolves that member's current ReturnKey and creates the resulting paragraph outside the ordered group membership; an absent or unresolved target is a consumed no-op.
  - **AC-WRITING-013.3 — Bounded FDX and spell-check ownership:** A supported dual source container owns its ordered member identities and wrapper provenance independently of the active left/right projection. Import recognizes the exact supported outer General/DualDialogue/spell-check topology separately from active member semantics and manages each safe nested Paragraph with one unique bounded nonempty exact Type, including arbitrary and case-distinct labels. Only the current exact-known two-cue/member pattern derives active sides; other supported member patterns remain valid inactive source state without an invented right cue. Source-backed and preservation-free writes retain the wrapper, all ordered members, content, identities, direct overrides, attachments, and opaque descendants, and reread compares the derived active split as well as source membership/provenance. Type changes do not unwrap the container or discard members. Unsupported wrapper topology remains opaque and does not fabricate a group or generalized nested story; genuinely unsafe or ambiguous managed association remains a bounded failure. The supported wrapper's geometry and spell-check data remain associated with the group; document-level spell-check data remains independently preserved. Unwrapping removes only group-owned spell-check data, and undo restores it. The application does not interpret spell-check ranges/words or provide a custom spell checker. Supported alternate dialogue data attached to a Dialogue remains opaque, preserved with that Dialogue, and is not treated as screenplay text, selection, layout content, or export content. Alternate data on unsupported type keys, surfaces, namespaces, or structures remains opaque where safely retainable and does not acquire alternate semantics.

Apply Template remaps every unmatched managed dual member's Type to the target's exact Action key without changing its wrapper, ordered member identities, member order, text/runs, direct overrides, attachments, or opaque source. The active left/right projection is recomputed from the resulting current definitions and member keys. If no supported active projection remains, the retained group is valid and lays out each member once in ordinary sequential order; Apply never unwraps, discards, synthesizes, or reorders the container.

Dual membership is source state, not a closed element-type vocabulary or a second active-side authority. The current registry's exact Character, Dialogue, and Parenthetical capabilities derive an active projection over the ordered source members; arbitrary keys and PaginateAs values do not acquire those capabilities. Generic type/definition changes and settings-only Apply preserve the source container and its members, while explicit disable remains the structural unwrap operation.

Because each built-in template retains the required base dual-dialogue definitions, applying a built-in normally leaves an already-active base-type group active. A source group that is already inactive remains retained and sequential when unmatched member keys map to Action; undo and redo derive the corresponding active or inactive projection from each restored registry/type state without changing source membership.

### Pagination and transient editor navigation

The Elements inspector reports current definition values and does not edit dual-group members or infer group membership from a definition summary. Apply-driven UI refresh observes the active projection derived from the resulting current registry and ordered source membership: an active group remains projected when its exact capability pattern is still valid, and an inactive retained group remains sequential with each member emitted once. The selector and its cancellation/error state never unwrap, discard, synthesize, or reorder a group.

  - **AC-WRITING-013.4 — Active and inactive projection geometry:** Only a group with a current active left/right projection uses two synchronized columns separated by a fixed shared gap, with equal widths derived from the current General definition's wrapper extent and its direct overrides; Character-capability cues center in each full column. Groups without an active projection remain source/document state but lay out each ordered member once in ordinary sequential body flow; they do not produce two-column geometry. Active columns use canonical document-point geometry and resolved per-scalar font metrics, not viewport zoom or desktop natural advances. Leading spacing before an active group is the maximum of the outer group and first visible member spacing, not their sum; fresh-page and column-top spacing rules remain consistent. An active block advances by the taller side, while source horizontal geometry remains retained for sequential editing. A group fitting on an empty printable page remains together; only an oversized active group may spill or split, with bounded forward progress.
  - **AC-WRITING-013.5 — Pagination:** A group fitting in the remaining printable vertical content bounds is placed whole; otherwise it moves to the next page when it fits there. Exact vertical extents and bounded forward progress determine fit; fixed occupied-line counts are diagnostic/compatibility data only and do not reject an otherwise fitting block. Members do not make separate keep-with-next decisions, and following content begins after the taller side. Only groups too large for an empty printable page use the deterministic bounded oversized fallback, which may spill/split but must progress without blank-page loops or unbounded retries. No MORE/CONT'D text is generated.
  - **AC-WRITING-013.6 — Transient editing and navigation:** At most one Body group is active for ordinary sequential editing at a time. Pointer selection in either inactive column selects the clicked position and activates that group on the following layout. Focus follows the selection even when it is noncollapsed; noncollapsed selection still cannot toggle a group. Moving down into a group from above selects the first Character at its beginning; moving up into it from below selects the end of the final right-side member. Left/Right across a group boundary use the corresponding first-member or final-right-member edge. Leaving by pointer, navigation, an outside caret (including Enter after the final right Dialogue), focus loss, surface/modal change, document replacement, or group removal clears active presentation without unwrapping the group. Changing modes resets preferred vertical position and stale caret/page geometry. Dotted boundaries appear only around the active Body group, remain clipped, and follow the active theme.

  For AC-WRITING-013.1–013.6, recognition, side derivation, and final-right-member handling use exact Character, Dialogue, and Parenthetical capabilities from current definitions. Active sides derive only over ordered membership already supported by the document's source container; a flat type-label pattern alone does not create active source membership. An explicit eligible toggle may create that source membership from the capability-recognized candidate. The final-right Enter operation resolves that member's current ReturnKey and creates the resulting paragraph outside the ordered group membership; it does not assume Action. A supported container retains its ordered source members while inactive, remains valid and editable in sequential flow, and is not unwrapped by a generic Type change or definition remap. Explicit disable remains the operation that unwraps an inactive container. Arbitrary/case-variant labels and PaginateAs values never act as dual members. Non-Content title structures remain opaque and are not dual-dialogue members.

### Output, commands, and alternate-data behavior

  - **AC-WRITING-013.8 — Toggle availability:** The Format menu provides a Dual Dialogue action for an eligible group, changing its label to Undual Dialogue when the current target is an existing group. The action is available only for Body content and follows the same eligibility and safe-edit behavior as direct editing. Command+D on macOS and Control+Alt+D on Windows toggle the group with one activation owner. Invalid selections, wrong surfaces, stale eligibility, extra/missing modifiers, key release, or unsupported platform bindings do not change the document. Edit does not provide an Edit Dual Dialogue item; Command+Shift+D is not a binding.
  - **AC-WRITING-013.9 — Alternate-data lifecycle and type safety:** Typing, paste, formatting, alignment, emptying text, and run edits retain supported alternate data on its surviving owner; canonical text/run edits may rebuild only affected Text projection while Alts remains opaque owner data. Split retains it only on the original/left Dialogue; the new right Dialogue receives none. Join, deletion, or removal of the owner removes its alternate data without transferring it. A legal type assignment away from Dialogue updates the canonical Type and current source Type without an Alts-based veto or automatic group unwrap; Alts remains opaque on that surviving owner. Deleting an owner does not delete the document's alternate collection; its entries remain, including orphaned copies. Undo/redo, Save/Save As, reopen, and save/rebase preserve the owner/source relationship.
  - **AC-WRITING-013.10 — Identity and preservation:** Alternate copies in the supported alternate collection retain their original identifiers and opaque content, including repeated copy identifiers. This exception does not allow duplicate managed paragraph identities, unsafe wrapper identities, or unrelated identity/reference conflicts. Saving and reopening preserve valid group content, alternate data, and source relationships; unsafe or unsupported structures are not silently rewritten. Print/export never exposes alternate copies or orphans.
  - **AC-WRITING-013.11 — Owner-scoped opaque data:** Generic opaque children and arbitrary unknown attributes, including Bookmark, remain bound to their exact supported dual member or outer wrapper and do not alter group membership, canonical text, layout, or exports. Member source follows the exact member node through wrap/unwrap and ordinary edits; outer source follows the wrapper lifetime and is removed with the wrapper on unwrap, never transferred to members. Member split retains opaque data/attributes only on the original/left owner; new owners receive none. Join retains only the designated survivor's own source; removed-owner source is discarded with its owner. Whole-owner deletion removes the owned subtree and attributes without a graveyard. Undo/redo restores the corresponding exact source snapshots. Unknown descendants and ID-like values do not create typed dual/paragraph identities or dependencies; managed identity and genuine external-reference checks remain strict. This contract is separate from `Alts` and spell-check data and does not broaden their supported placements. Bookmark navigation, editing, and semantic behavior are not supported.

  For AC-WRITING-013.9–013.11, only the exact Dialogue capability gives `Alts` its supported alternate-data association; a Type change never deletes or transfers that opaque owner data. Generated Parenthetical behavior is governed by the exact Parenthetical capability and session-only provenance, not by punctuation, a Behavior target, or an arbitrary label. Unknown attachments remain opaque on their surviving owner, and inactive dual source membership remains independent of these projections.
