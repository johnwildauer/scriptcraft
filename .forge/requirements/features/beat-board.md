# Requirements Area: Beat Board

## Area purpose

The focused Beat Board gives a writer a separate spatial workspace for independent cards without changing the screenplay or its title page.

## Approved target contract

### Semantic-authority and WO-008 text-transaction clarification

During edits the semantic Beat board and its associated identity/graph state are authoritative. Card creation, activation and final geometry changes publish atomically through session history; retained source bytes and XML nodes are not a second live authority and are reconciled by the writer only on save. Unrelated source remains preserved. This is source integration only; validation belongs to the user.

Card and text selection are session-local; selection, caret, and typing-state changes do not dirty the document or create history. Hit-testing uses reverse Item order; dragging margins and selected edges/corners uses fractional transient previews, with one final semantic transaction on release and Escape cancellation. Creation uses a 240 × 100 rectangle, durable identities and an empty paragraph. Final geometry uses nearest 20 pixels with nonnegative half-up ties, width 120–1000 and height 60–1000, and clamps the whole rectangle to the finite board; resizing retains opposite-edge intent.

WO-008 supplies headless title/body text transactions. Title remains a plain single-line string; typed insertion rejects CR/LF, paste converts line breaks to spaces, and Enter moves selection to Body paragraph 0. Body is an ordered paragraph sequence with canonical UTF-8 byte positions, ordered formatted runs, cross-paragraph selection, LF-delimited plain-text projection, split/join/delete, normalized paste, and logical paragraph-edge/horizontal navigation. Every created paragraph receives fresh session and durable identity at creation. Left/surviving paragraphs retain their identity and source-owned data; new split/paste paragraphs receive supported defaults only. One semantic candidate, identity graph update, history entry, revision, and dirty recomputation publish together; no-op/failure leaves state unchanged. FDX source projection occurs on Save, not during editing. Desktop input/IME/clipboard routing belongs to WO-009; inline formatting controls belong to WO-010.

- **REQ-BEAT-001 — Board and source ownership:** The first valid direct-root Beat display board is the presentation winner. Later Beat duplicates remain inert on Open and are removed only by the next transactional Save or Save As; their independent ListItems and other board types remain preserved. Open alone does not dirty the document. This is a narrow, explicit exception to the general policy against silently resolving board conflicts through deletion.
- **REQ-BEAT-002 — Cards and identities:** The board owns cards in winning Item back-to-front order, while ListItems retain source/creation order. Each card has a durable ListItem identity, a plain single-line title, a rectangle, and one or more ordered rich-text body paragraphs. Each paragraph has a session-local stable identity, optional durable paragraph identity, canonical text and ordered runs. Stable card and paragraph identities are distinct from all other Project owners. Empty bodies contain one empty paragraph with no runs; placeholders are presentation only.
- **REQ-BEAT-003 — Geometry:** The default finite board is 24,000 × 10,000 logical pixels and a new card is 240 × 100. Committed cards measure 120–1000 pixels wide and 60–1000 pixels high and remain within the board. User commits snap to 20-pixel increments; creation anchors the default rectangle's top-left at the snapped and clamped pointer. Fractional imported geometry is represented exactly to thousandths of a pixel and unusable input is normalized before semantic validation.
- **REQ-BEAT-004 — Editing:** Titles are plain Arial 12 bold and single-line; title Enter moves to the body and pasted title line breaks become spaces. Body paragraphs support splitting, joining, cross-paragraph selection, and the existing inline Style, Font, Size, Color, and Highlight controls. Semantic changes are transactional and reversible; failed and no-op operations do not partially mutate the document or session.
- **REQ-BEAT-005 — State separation:** Board identity, dimensions, card order, card content, and geometry are semantic document state. Zoom, scroll origin, selection, caret, hover, IME, gesture previews, fonts, and layout caches are disposable view or session state. Board navigation alone is not a semantic edit. The Beat root view is independent of screenplay Body and Title Page, and Beat content is excluded from PDF, plain-text, and native print output.
- **REQ-BEAT-006 — Boundaries:** Card deletion, Story Map editing, non-Beat item editing, broader outlining views, and board export are excluded. This initiative adds no permanent tests or test-only fixtures.

### Finite viewport and navigation (WO-005)

The Beat viewport occupies the central workspace without a scrollbar. Its finite board is top-left anchored, including when smaller than the viewport; the outside uses the workspace background. The board uses the document dimensions, defaulting to 24,000 × 10,000. A 40-board-pixel grid has its first horizontal and vertical lines at 20 pixels, preserving half cells at the top and left; only lines intersecting the clipped visible board are painted.

With zoom factor `z = zoom_milli_percent / 100000`, projection is `screen = viewport_origin + (board - scroll_origin) * z` and its inverse is `board = scroll_origin + (screen - viewport_origin) / z`. Each origin axis is bounded from zero through `max(0, board_dimension - viewport_dimension / z)` on composition, resize, pan and zoom. Wheel and trackpad scrolling preserve both axes and Shift-to-horizontal behavior; there is no overscroll. Controls anchor zoom at the viewport center, while pinch and zoom-wheel anchor at the pointer's board point when available and otherwise at the center. Clamping takes precedence at board edges.

Origin and zoom are disposable per-document values, not Project edits: they do not change dirty state, revision, mutation token or undo history. Transactional Save and Save As capture the current clamped values; successful activation installs imported state and the first Beat composition applies the viewport-dependent upper clamp. Failed activation or save does not replace live view state. Switching roots retains the document's Beat values and clears transient geometry. WO-005 did not introduce cards; read-only card rendering subsequently landed in WO-006, while interactions remain later work.

### Read-only card presentation (WO-006 source integration)

Imported cards use non-paginated, left-aligned multi-paragraph text flow shared with the existing measured wrapping machinery, independently of screenplay definitions and page composition. The title uses Arial 12 bold and the body Arial 12 regular, with supported body run formatting resolved through the prepared font environment. Font preparation includes Beat run families, title/body scalars and uppercase expansions, placeholder and ellipsis scalars, alongside the existing neutral and replacement requirements.

Empty titles and wholly empty bodies show subdued “Beat Title” and “Body” placeholders. A long title uses a display-only ellipsis, while its full source and layout remain intact. Body paint stops before the first line that does not fit wholly in the body region; all paragraphs and canonical text remain intact. Cards use page-themed fill and border, paint in winning Item back-to-front order, and are culled to the visible board. Layouts are disposable and keyed by card content, dimensions and metrics, and the resolved font environment; moving a card or zooming does not change its card-local layout. Beat content remains excluded from screenplay page layout, PDF, plain text, and native print.

One board pixel is one unscaled logical point for card text layout. Whole-pixel sizes are exact; an existing fractional imported width or height is rounded to the nearest hundredth only for text layout. The durable rectangle is neither rounded nor snapped by presentation. WO-007 supplies selection, creation, movement and resizing; WO-008 supplies headless text transactions, while desktop caret, keyboard, clipboard and IME routing remains WO-009. This records source integration only, not rendered behavior or acceptance; validation is user-owned.

### Desktop text editing and IME (WO-009 source integration)

Beat text focus is desktop-transient and separate from Script/Title editor focus. On a selected card, Title and Body content rectangles map pointer coordinates through the board transform and the WO-006 full canonical layout; title hit-testing uses the full line plus title-follow offset, and Body uses canonical paragraph/line ranges plus the focused vertical offset. Click/drag selection dispatches command 68 only; Unicode word double-click and current-paragraph/title triple-click retain canonical byte boundaries. The active Text selection keeps the card stroke, and card spatial gestures do not replace an active text selection until an explicit card/margin/empty-board action.

Selection backgrounds and the focused collapsed caret are painted from canonical title/body positions inside title/body/card/viewport clips. Empty placeholders never become source ranges; empty-field clicks resolve to the valid zero position. Long focused titles follow the caret horizontally without changing content; focused overflowing bodies scroll vertically on wheel/trackpad before board pan, and caret reveal adjusts the transient offset. Body drag beyond its vertical content bounds auto-scrolls while selection extends. Blur, Escape, card/root/document changes reset offsets and discard IME preedit.

While the Beat root owns focused text, ordered Text/Paste/Key/IME events route to commands 68–70 and WO-008 session operations. Enter splits Body paragraphs or moves Title focus to Body; Title insertion stays single-line and pasted line breaks become spaces. Home/End use visual lines, Up/Down target adjacent laid-out lines with a preferred x, and left/right retain session paragraph crossing. Tab/control characters are not inserted. Existing global Copy/Cut/Paste/Delete/Select All actions are available only for focused Beat Text context and use the exact selected plain-text projection; Cut mutates only after clipboard write succeeds. Undo/Redo remain shared-session commands. Card-only selection never invokes text clipboard or deletion behavior. Inline formatting/chrome is WO-010 and is not part of this order.

IME Preedit is transient and paint-only; Commit dispatches one Insert or multiline Paste, with duplicate matching Text suppression. Enabled/Disabled clear or update Beat-local IME state. The current egui viewport API in this source integration has no custom candidate-window rectangle command, so publishing the transformed caret rectangle to a native IME anchor remains an explicit platform-adapter gap. No physical IME, accessibility, visual, or runtime acceptance is implied; validation remains user-owned.

### Inline formatting and shared controls (WO-010 source integration)

Formatting commands 71–73 target only a valid, focused Beat Body Text selection. Bold, Italics, Underline, All Caps, and Strikethrough use the existing uniform all-on/all-off range rule across all intersected paragraphs; Plain Text clears only those supported style flags. Font, Size, Color, and Highlight use the existing bounded typed patch and exact shared palette. At a collapsed caret, style and patch changes update session-only Beat typing state consumed by subsequent insertion; they do not change Project, revision, history, dirty state, or saved data. A noncollapsed range is transformed across its actual paragraph edges in one detached Project candidate, preserving text, unknown Style tokens, unrelated attributes, and every unselected prefix/suffix. Actual changes publish one history/revision/dirty update; no-op and invalid ranges publish nothing. Undo/redo restores semantic runs together with Beat selection and typing state.

The shared formatting menu and configured Styles toolbar group project checks and enablement from the currently focused Beat Body selection. Style, Font, Size, Color, and Highlight are available for collapsed and noncollapsed Body selections; Title, Card-only and None selections disable inline actions. Beat Alignment and other screenplay-only formatting remain unavailable. Existing Script and Title Page formatting behavior is unchanged. Beat Highlight remains durable/editable Background run data but is not newly painted or emitted by Beat rendering/output; the active shared Background rendering/output exclusion continues to apply. This is source integration only, not visual or native-menu acceptance.

### FDX Open projection

The first direct root-namespace DisplayBoards container supplies the first direct same-namespace DisplayBoard with exactly one unqualified Type equal to Beat. Only its direct same-namespace Items participate, in stacking order. Their references resolve against direct ListItems in the first direct root-namespace ListItems container; only exact Beat ListItems are projected. Unreferenced, non-Beat, later-board, foreign-namespace, and opaque-ancestry material remains retained and unprojected. Required dangling references and ambiguous identities remain errors.

For each projected ListItem, Title is source text, and the first direct same-namespace Content supplies direct same-namespace Paragraphs and direct Text runs in source order. Missing Content or Paragraphs yields one empty semantic paragraph without changing source. Recognized direct paragraph settings and optional foreign paragraph IDs are retained in the projection; card and paragraph stable IDs are independently allocated and do not replace durable source identities.

Missing or unusable board dimensions use 24,000 × 10,000 defaults; imported rectangles use 240 × 100 fallbacks and are bounded to the card limits and board. Empty or malformed view state defaults to origin 0,0 and zoom 100%; finite zoom is clamped to 5–300%, and scroll coordinates are bounded with viewport-specific upper clamping deferred until composition. Open does not rewrite source, create history, or dirty the document. Duplicate-board removal is a later transactional-save obligation, not an Open action.

### FDX output and rebase

The managed Beat board, card titles, geometry, ordered paragraphs, settings, canonical text and runs are semantic document data and directly determine their FDX projection. Editing does not require reconstructing original Text occurrences. New cards and paragraphs receive collision-checked durable identities in their creation transaction; surviving identities do not change on text edits, moves or Save As. A coordinate used to bind an imported id-less paragraph is not a durable selection identity. ListItems creation order is independent of Item stacking, and unrelated source and graph data remain retained. Transactional Save and Save As alone normalize later Beat duplicates and capture disposable board view state; direct source-backed serialization retains existing duplicate boards and view values. Complete semantic, identity, graph and normalized-source comparisons precede publication. Rebase uses durable card and paragraph identity, with only the bounded unambiguous id-less fallback permitted by the approved plan.
