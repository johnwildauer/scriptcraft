# ScriptCraft Product Overview

## The product and its audience

ScriptCraft is a focused desktop writing tool for individual screenwriters. Writers need to shape and revise a screenplay without fighting a general-purpose document editor, losing screenplay structure, or wondering whether their saved draft is dependable. ScriptCraft keeps the writing experience centered on the screenplay and gives the writer a clear path from draft to shareable copy.

## What a writer can expect

A writer can create and edit screenplay scenes using the element types and editing behavior defined by the current document. Unfamiliar element types remain editable rather than being silently converted to a default type. Writers can navigate and revise the draft and undo or redo changes. The editor presents a readable screenplay page view so writers can work with both text and page flow. Formatting stays focused on screenplay writing rather than attempting to be a general word processor.

A title page can be edited alongside the screenplay while remaining a distinct part of the document. ScriptCraft presents the screenplay and title page in a desktop workspace, with document actions available as part of the writing flow.

Writers can create a new document from the Screenplay, Stageplay, or Dramatists Guild Musical template, retaining that template's editable example body and title-page content. The read-only Elements inspector presents the current document's ordered definitions; Apply a Template changes only element definitions/settings and maps unmatched managed types to Action while preserving current writing. Opening an external FDXT creates new untitled content and does not make the template source a save target. Writers can open, save, and reopen native screenplay documents, retaining supported screenplay content and document information. They can also produce PDF page-layout copies and deliberately simpler plain-text copies. These exports serve different purposes and do not replace the editable native document. The desktop experience is designed for one writer working with a project at a time.

## Success outcomes

- Writers can move from creating a screenplay to revising, saving, reopening, and sharing it in one focused workflow.
- Screenplay structure remains useful and recognizable throughout editing and native-document round trips.
- Page presentation helps writers anticipate how a draft reads on the page without making a promise of identical rendering in every screenplay application.
- Writers can tell which copy remains editable and what information may be lost in an export.
- Routine work feels dependable: edits are reversible, and document operations protect the writer's latest valid work.

## Product boundaries

**In scope:** screenplay-focused writing and revision; title-page editing; readable page presentation; native screenplay document open/save; PDF and plain-text exports; and the desktop workspace needed to use these capabilities. Bounded support for dual dialogue and conversion from Fountain text are included as focused writing workflows.

**Out of scope:** general-purpose word processing; guaranteed compatibility or pixel-perfect rendering for every screenplay file or application; collaboration, cloud services, and revision tracking; production planning and scheduling; broad outlining or beat-board tools; and web-based editing. Unsupported source structures may be retained without becoming editable product features; unfamiliar element-type labels alone do not make managed paragraphs opaque.
