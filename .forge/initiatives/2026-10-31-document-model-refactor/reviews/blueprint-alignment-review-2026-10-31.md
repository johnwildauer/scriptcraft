- **Outcome: PASS WITH WARNINGS.** The target architecture and staged work-order plan are materially aligned for the bounded FDX document-model initiative. **Blockers to blueprint alignment or the WO-001 evidence investigation: none identified.** This finding is not design review, requirements approval, human approval, implementation validation, or Final Draft interoperability evidence.

## Findings

1. **Architecture and work-order sequence align.** The target keeps the semantic model in the headless core, separates the FDX codec from filesystem persistence, and couples managed fields with ordered source-preservation state ([technical context](../../../blueprints/technical-context.md#L22-L49)). The plan stages evidence and mapping first, then model and preservation/edit invariants, codec, migration, storage, desktop open/save cutover, JSON sunset/recovery, and integrated evidence ([initiative plan](../document-model-refactor-v1.md#L197-L222)). WO-001 is appropriately investigation-only and requires evidence or escalation before dependent implementation ([WO-001](../work-orders/WO-001-mapping-and-safety-evidence.md#L3-L25)); the transitional JSON guard in WO-002 and final retirement in WO-013 do not promise lossy JSON migration ([WO-002](../work-orders/WO-002-content-model.md#L13-L25), [WO-013](../work-orders/WO-013-json-sunset-and-recovery.md#L6-L24)).

2. **Unsupported FDX content is treated consistently with the user decisions.** The architecture and work orders cover ordered unknown XML, source identity/anchors, runs, title-page structure, nested scene data, and board/item/image payloads and internal links, without pretending these are editable features or claiming byte identity or universal compatibility ([technical context](../../../blueprints/technical-context.md#L43-L49); [WO-003](../work-orders/WO-003-preservation-state.md#L6-L22); [WO-006](../work-orders/WO-006-fdx-v6-reader.md#L6-L21); [WO-007](../work-orders/WO-007-fdx-v6-writer.md#L6-L22)). This respects the explicit boundaries: title-page editing is deferred; script and boards are independent; cross-domain links may be orphaned silently; board entities and internal relationships are not thereby authorized for deletion ([requirements](../../../requirements/features/screenplay-writing-loop.md#L124-L134); [initiative decisions](../document-model-refactor-v1.md#L263-L278)). The older-version title/content-only conversion and General fallback are explicit exceptions, not a general license to coerce Version 6 unknown types ([WO-008](../work-orders/WO-008-version-migration.md#L6-L21)).

3. **Safety and migration boundaries are carried into the sequence.** WO-001 owns parser, mapping, and budget evidence; the reader requires bounded secure parsing and safe failure; migration distinguishes explicit non-6 values from absent/invalid/duplicate declarations and stages a candidate; storage and cutover preserve destination, source, session, path, and clean baseline on failure ([WO-001](../work-orders/WO-001-mapping-and-safety-evidence.md#L6-L19); [WO-006](../work-orders/WO-006-fdx-v6-reader.md#L13-L19); [WO-008](../work-orders/WO-008-version-migration.md#L13-L21); [WO-010](../work-orders/WO-010-atomic-fdx-storage.md#L6-L21); [WO-011](../work-orders/WO-011-native-fdx-open.md#L13-L21); [WO-012](../work-orders/WO-012-native-fdx-save-cutover.md#L13-L22)). The blueprint and plan correctly leave concrete mappings, budgets, parser capabilities, and real-app compatibility as evidence to obtain—not established facts.

4. **Fountain is correctly deferred.** The target places Fountain in a separate secondary adapter, not native persistence ([technical context](../../../blueprints/technical-context.md#L26), [requirements](../../../requirements/features/screenplay-writing-loop.md#L162-L168)). The current sequence contains no Fountain implementation order, and WO-014 expressly excludes its implementation ([initiative plan](../document-model-refactor-v1.md#L18-L20); [WO-014](../work-orders/WO-014-integrated-compatibility-evidence.md#L13-L19)). This is consistent with the user's decision that Fountain conversion follows completion of the FDX model; its no-mandatory-loss-warning rule is not displaced by older research proposals.
