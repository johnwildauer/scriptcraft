# V2-WO-001.2 — Viewport-centered scroll compensation

- **Status:** Implementation submitted 2026-11-04; user validation pending
- **Initiative:** [`ui-quality-of-life-v2-zoom-and-typing.md`](../ui-quality-of-life-v2-zoom-and-typing.md)

- **Validation owner:** User; no agent-run checks or independent validators

## Objective

Keep the captured viewport-center document point stationary across zoom by applying a one-shot compensated scroll offset after new page geometry exists.

## Scope

1. In [`live_document_surface()`](../../apps/scriptcraft/src/main.rs:1348), resolve the pending anchor against newly allocated page rectangles and current scale.
2. Apply horizontal and vertical compensation exactly once through the appropriate egui scroll mechanism. Account for constant [`PAGE_VERTICAL_GAP`](../../apps/scriptcraft/src/main.rs) and horizontal padding rather than uniformly scaling every content coordinate.
3. Clamp at new scroll extents, consume the pending request only after successful geometry publication, and avoid a repeated correction on subsequent frames.
4. Keep manual scrolling and [`RevealIntent`](../../apps/scriptcraft/src/main.rs:357) independent. Zoom must not create a caret reveal or change selection.
5. Preserve current behavior when no valid anchor exists and on document replacement.
6. Add desktop oracle coverage for zoom-in/out at the beginning, middle, and end; 75–300% bounds; repeated pinch; slider/preset/keyboard parity; horizontal overflow; narrow viewports; gap anchors; edge clamping; and caret independence. Tests are authored, not agent-run.

## Boundaries

- Do not alter fixed document layout, page count, canonical text, native view-state persistence percentages, or exports.
- Do not introduce pointer/caret anchoring, animated zoom, or asynchronous rendering.
- Do not implement derived-refresh work or later v2 orders.

## Structural acceptance

- The anchored page-local point remains at viewport center within geometry rounding tolerance, except at clamped edges.
- Both axes compensate correctly and constant page gaps remain constant.
- One zoom request causes one correction, not a feedback loop or drifting offset.
- Zoom leaves session/layout/export state unchanged.
