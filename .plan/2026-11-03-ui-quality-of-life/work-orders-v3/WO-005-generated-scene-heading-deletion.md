## Objective

Allow a structural edit to remove a Scene Heading together with its recognized app-owned generated SceneProperties/SceneArcBeats subtree, while continuing to reject unknown attachments and unsafe identities/references.

## Scope

1. Add a read-only classifier for the exact supported generated scene-container shape. Reuse the recognition semantics in [`recognized_beat()`](../../../crates/scriptcraft-core/src/scene_metadata.rs:761) and the generated-container contract in [`generated_scene_properties()`](../../../crates/scriptcraft-core/src/scene_metadata.rs:687); do not create a second conflicting definition of supported arcs.
2. In [`remove_managed_paragraph()`](../../../crates/scriptcraft-core/src/preservation.rs:1997), keep the existing plain-paragraph branch and add a Scene Heading-only generated-container branch. Do not relax [`is_plain_managed_paragraph()`](../../../crates/scriptcraft-core/src/preservation.rs:2708) globally.
3. Before mutation, collect the managed heading's foreign Paragraph ID and every recognized nested arc Paragraph ID. Require unambiguous ownership, current registration, and safe removal with no known reference or external source use for every identity. Any duplicate, unknown child, unsupported arc payload, missing identity, or reference conflict rejects the detached candidate.
4. On success, remove the complete heading subtree, unregister exactly the collected nested and heading identities, remove associated managed anchors/direct overrides, and rebuild affected transient indexes through the existing transactional candidate boundary. Do not remove Cast Lists or unrelated body/source nodes.
5. Author focused coverage for an empty generated arc, a supported generated arc with retained nested Text/runs, and a cross-paragraph selection deletion that removes the heading without invoking split/join. Include undo/redo and serialization/reopen. Retain unknown-attachment, duplicate-beat, external-reference, unsafe-identity, backspace/join, and split safe-stop controls.
6. Clarify the deletion-only ownership rule in [`screenplay-writing-loop.md`](../../../.forge/requirements/features/screenplay-writing-loop.md:292), [`product-overview.md`](../../../.forge/requirements/product-overview.md:32), and [`technical-context.md`](../../../.forge/blueprints/technical-context.md:203) without weakening the explicit split/join safe-stop.

## Boundaries

- Do not permit role changes, split, or join to discard or rewrite attached scene data as a side effect.
- Do not delete unsupported authored arcs, opaque siblings, referenced nested Paragraphs, or any content outside the removed heading subtree.
- Do not change derived refresh, Cast List correction, Scene Heading text synchronization, or native save gates.

## Structural acceptance

- A recognized generated empty or supported nonempty scene subtree can be removed with its heading through an otherwise valid cross-paragraph selection deletion.
- Every removed nested identity is cleaned up consistently; undo/redo restores the exact prior subtree and identities.
- Every unknown or unsafe control rejects with no project, selection, history, dirty-state, index, or source changes.
