## Implementation handoff — 2026-11-01

The active edit-time cue-uppercasing policy has been removed. Insert/paste, split, role assignment, Tab, and existing edit/history paths now leave canonical text casing unchanged; the runtime `EditPolicy` no longer exposes `uppercase_cues`. The unused preservation-layer uppercase mutation helper was removed. Existing layout AllCaps and Unicode display-to-source mapping remain unchanged. Imported role/source synchronization and transactional editing are retained, including the prior Scene Heading attachment and ambiguous imported-arc safe-stops; no attempt was made to broaden preservation ownership.

Focused regression cases replace the old canonical-uppercase expectations and cover all shared-default AllCaps roles (Scene Heading, Character, Transition, Shot, Cast List, New Act, Sequence, End of Act), mixed-case edits through split/join/delete and undo/redo, expanding Unicode display mapping, new and imported FDX round-trips, a FontSpec override without AllCaps, and legacy JSON's `uppercase_cues` compatibility field independently of FDX runtime behavior. Tests were authored only; no tests, builds, lint, formatting, manual verification, implementation checks, reviews, or other validation were run. The user owns validation under the authoritative manifest policy.

Durable updates are in [AC-WRITING-001.2](../../../.forge/requirements/features/screenplay-writing-loop.md:24), [the product overview](../../../.forge/requirements/product-overview.md:20), and [technical context](../../../.forge/blueprints/technical-context.md:43). No export code or claim about exported text casing is included in this order.
