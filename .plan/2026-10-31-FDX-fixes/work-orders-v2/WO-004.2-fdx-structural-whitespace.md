- **Status:** Implementation authored; user review and validation pending
- **Depends on:** [V2-WO-004.1](WO-004.1-reference-section-order.md)
- **Validation:** User-owned; no agent-run checks

## Outcome

Format generated FDX like the references and keep newly inserted paragraphs structurally readable without changing canonical text.

## Scope

- Emit LF returns, two spaces per indentation level, blank lines between FinalDraft children and ElementSettings blocks, no blank lines between Content paragraphs, and one final return.
- Give newly inserted or split managed paragraphs appropriate Content sibling whitespace.
- Never rewrite whitespace inside Text, CDATA, comments, processing instructions, or opaque mixed content. Do not globally pretty-print imported unknown structures.
- Apply any detached formatting normalization equally to the expected structural comparison tree in [`write_v6()`](../../../crates/scriptcraft-core/src/fdx.rs:372); do not weaken re-read validation.

## Regression coverage

Compare exact generated whitespace with the reference convention, including SceneProperties and nested arc paragraphs. Reopen generated output, insert/split paragraphs, save again, and verify coherent formatting plus unchanged canonical whitespace and opaque content.

## Durable records

Update [native persistence requirements](../../../.forge/requirements/features/screenplay-writing-loop.md:136) and [the technical context](../../../.forge/blueprints/technical-context.md:43) with the generated-structure-only formatting boundary.

## Implementation handoff — 2026-11-02

The writer now formats newly generated ScriptCraft element-only structure with LF returns, two ASCII spaces per indentation level, blank lines between direct FinalDraft children and adjacent ElementSettings, no blank lines between Content paragraphs, and one final LF. It preserves the declaration and generated Content → 17 native-order ElementSettings → optional TitlePage order. Formatting is added as explicit structural nodes and does not touch text-bearing Text elements, CDATA, comments, PIs, or mixed content. Generated SceneProperties and nested scene-arc paragraphs receive the same indentation when their content is unambiguously element-only.

For imported documents, only direct Content sibling whitespace adjacent to newly inserted/split managed Paragraphs may be normalized. This path is limited to Content made exclusively of Paragraph elements and whitespace-only sibling text, without effective `xml:space="preserve"`. It formats the affected gap(s), not imported top-level sections or opaque subtrees. The detached expected source tree includes the same explicit whitespace edits before comparison; re-read tree comparison, semantic checks, identity checks, and atomic save behavior remain intact. If new/split paragraph formatting is required but Content is mixed, structurally ambiguous, or under effective `xml:space="preserve"`, serialization returns `FdxError::UnsupportedConstruct` rather than guessing, changing source content, or emitting an unformatted managed insertion. In those contexts Save remains safely refused and prior source/session state is not published.

Focused regressions were authored in [`wo0042_fdx_structural_whitespace.rs`](../../../crates/scriptcraft-core/tests/wo0042_fdx_structural_whitespace.rs) for generated reference-style whitespace including nested scene arcs, exact canonical tabs/LF/CR and whitespace-only Text round-trip, split/reopen/resave sibling formatting, and refusal in mixed-content/`xml:space` contexts.
